Benchmark methodology

Measured at the screen.

The question is simple: when does the person at the keyboard actually see a new photo?

  1. 01

    Real pixels, not internal timers

    DXGI Desktop Duplication observes the rendered desktop. A photo counts only when a meaningful part of the image region changes, so a spinner or toolbar cannot win the test.

  2. 02

    Clean launch, repeated

    Cold-open time starts before the app launches and ends on the first visible photo. Each cold-start and RAW opening result is the median of 20 runs.

  3. 03

    Like-for-like browsing

    Navigation starts at the key press and uses the same pixel-change rule. The file sequences are boundary-safe, deterministic where needed, and every comparable app uses the same fixtures.

Test system: Windows 11 Pro · Ryzen 5 5600 · RTX 3060 Ti · 32 GB RAM · 2560×1440 / 180 Hz. Apps were launched explicitly, equivalent to choosing them with “Open with”.

VetroLook Privacy Policy

Your photos
stay yours.

VetroLook is made to work with the folders already on your PC — without an account, cloud library or advertising profile.

  1. 01

    Files stay on your PC

    VetroLook opens and indexes the folders you choose. Your images, folders and metadata are not uploaded to a VetroLook server.

  2. 02

    No account or analytics

    The app does not require an account and does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers to profile your photo library.

  3. 03

    Website connections

    This website may ask GitHub for the latest VetroLook release. Links to GitHub, Telegram and Ko-fi open services governed by their own privacy policies.

  4. 04

    Questions

    For a privacy question or request, contact the project through GitHub Issues or the Vetro Telegram chat.
