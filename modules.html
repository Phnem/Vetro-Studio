Chunked downloadSeveral connections pull 1 MB chunks from one shared queue: it resumes from the first missing chunk, replaces slow connections and re-resolves expired links.
  • Single stream47.3 s
  • 6 equal parts9.8 s
  • 6 connections, chunk queue10.2 s

The classic equal split is within noise of the chunk queue on this network: the speed-up comes from parallelism, while the queue’s value is resilience — resume, slow-connection replacement and no stitching pass. Your network will differ.
