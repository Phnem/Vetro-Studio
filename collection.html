Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Vetro respects your privacy. It is local-first: your collection is stored on the device. A Vetro account enables sync and backup. Network requests happen when you use online search, media sources, sync or backup, updates, or an optional AI provider.

Local data

Most application data is stored locally on your device and stays under your control.
