A library that knows your folders

MediaStore scanning plus extra folders through the system picker. Directory trees, grid and list layouts, thumbnails, search, sorting and filters.

Pick up where you left off

Favorites, hidden folders, history, resume playback, playlists and a queue.

03

A player with gestures

AndroidX Media3 / ExoPlayer with gesture controls, speed, zoom and aspect ratio, track selection, background playback and picture-in-picture.

04

Subtitles, done properly

Embedded and external tracks, SRT and VTT, styled ASS/SSA through libass, two at once, timing offsets, text editing and export.

05

Your sources, anywhere

HTTP(S), HLS, DASH, SMB, WebDAV, FTP/FTPS and SFTP, plus Jellyfin, Emby, Plex and browsing DLNA sources.

06

Audio and video tools

Processing settings, external audio tracks, audio synchronization, screenshots and clip export. On a TV, play over DLNA, or over Google Cast in the cast build.

On-device AI

Speech in.
Subtitles out.

Generate subtitles with Whisper, translate them between English and Russian locally, and let Smart Sync line them up. Models are downloaded once; after that it all works offline.

Video→Whisper→Subtitles→Translate→Smart Sync
01

Whisper

On-device subtitle generation after you download a model, with chunked processing of finite videos, caching and cancellation.

02

Local translation

EN → RU and RU → EN through CTranslate2 on your device. Optional cloud translation uses a compatible API and your own key.

03

Smart Sync

Estimates subtitle offsets and uniform timing drift from recognized speech.

Whisper and translation models are downloaded separately and are not bundled in the APK. Cloud translation sends text to the service you configure.

Good to know

Two builds.
Honest limits.

01

Pick your build

foss is the main build without the Google Cast SDK; it still includes DLNA. cast adds Google Cast. Both use the same package and replace each other.

02

Android 8.0 and newer

Universal APKs for arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86_64 and x86. Updating an installation signed with the same key keeps your data.

03

What it does not do

Codec, HDR and export support depend on your device. There is no universal transcoding for TV receivers and no live subtitle streaming. VAP provides no movies or servers.

VAP Privacy Policy

Your videos
stay yours.

VAP is a free player with no ads and no accounts. It works with the videos already on your device and the sources you add yourself.

  1. 01

    Your videos stay on your device

    VAP scans the folders you allow and keeps its library, history, playlists and settings on the device. None of it is uploaded to a VAP server — VAP has no servers.

  2. 02

    No ads, no accounts, no analytics

    VAP is free to use and shows no ads. It needs no account and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

  3. 03

    Network only when you use it

    VAP goes online only for features you choose: playing from network sources and media servers you configure, subtitle search through OpenSubtitles with your own key, downloading Whisper and translation models, and optional cloud translation through an API you set up, which receives the subtitle text. Saved connection details and keys stay on your device.

  4. 04

    Permissions

    VAP asks for access to videos on your device, notifications for playback and background processing, and network access. Extra folders are added through the system folder picker. The cast build also includes the Google Cast SDK, which is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

  5. 05

    Website and questions

    This website may ask GitHub for the latest VAP release. Links to GitHub, Telegram and other services open sites with their own privacy policies. For a privacy question, contact the project through GitHub Issues or the Vetro Telegram chat.
